through a task — read them when you want the model in your head, not a recipe.

- **[Mental model](mental-model.md)** — what a workspace is, the reactive inbox
  model, and why lazybox isolates one worktree and one agent per task.
- **[Architecture](architecture.md)** — the client/daemon split, the crates, the
  event bus, the credential chain, and the embedded terminal.
